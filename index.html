How the intelligence is built

The interface receives an allowlisted product payload, never the private database schema.

1 · Backbone

Establishment first

DIFE observations remain source-specific and versioned. Licence expiry is not treated as closure.

2 · Resolution

Site ≠ organization

External records are linked only with explicit name, geography and address evidence. Ambiguity remains visible.

3 · Intelligence

Evidence before inference

Products, capacity, employment, export and environmental fields retain their source and vintage.
